# 📊 PC Hardware Sales & Profitability Dashboard (Power BI)

An executive Business Intelligence dashboard analyzing PC hardware sales, processor margins, and regional demand across configurations.

## 🚀 Key Business Insights
- **Gaming & Workstation Lead:** Gaming PCs drive over ₹2.6 Cr in revenue, delivering high net profit margins.
- **Hardware Shift (SSD Dominance):** NVMe SSD configurations account for 74.2% of revenue share, while legacy HDD demand fell to 9.2%.
- **Top Processors:** Intel Core i9-14900K and AMD Ryzen 7 7800X3D are the largest margin contributors.
- **Recommendation:** Phased inventory reduction for legacy HDD stock and reallocation of marketing spend toward high-spec gaming builds.

## 🛠️ Tech Stack
- **Tools:** Power BI Desktop, DAX, Excel, Data Modeling
- **Metrics:** Total Revenue (₹7.07 Cr), Net Profit (₹1.09 Cr), Profit Margin (15.53%), Units Sold (883)
